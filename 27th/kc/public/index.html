THE CODE OF WAR

Tournament Rules

01 · Tournament Window

Matches are locked until the admin starts the tournament. The event lasts 48 hours; new challenges close when it ends.

02 · 10 Kingdoms

30 approved players maximum, with 3 player slots per kingdom. Each kingdom begins with 15 territories: 150 total.

03 · Match Timers

15 minutes for the defending side to accept. Once accepted, both players have 5 minutes to check in. The match unlocks only after both check in.

04 · Defense Points

Each territory starts with 3 Defense Points. An unanswered/declined challenge removes 1 point. At zero, an unanswered challenge can lead to automatic occupation. Points regenerate by 1 per hour while the tournament is active, up to 3.

05 · Kingdom Attacks

Multiple matches may run simultaneously. A kingdom must challenge a different kingdom before attacking its previous opponent again. Defending against the same kingdom repeatedly is allowed.

06 · Match Allowance

Each player may start up to 5 matches per personal two-hour period. A player cannot participate in multiple active matches at once.

07 · Territory Targeting

The same attacking kingdom cannot target the same territory again. Each territory can be challenged only while it has no unresolved challenge.

08 · Peace Time

The admin can start a ten-hour Peace Time. New challenges are blocked, the player map shows a live countdown, Defense Points continue regenerating, and the 48-hour tournament timer keeps running.

09 · Results & Fair Play

Both players report results. Matching reports finalize automatically; conflicting reports go to the admin. Exploits and false reports may result in penalties.
